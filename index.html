01 / A little about me

How I think & what I do

I've always enjoyed problems that take more than one step to solve. Logic puzzles, strategy, and working through a complicated question appeal to me because the most useful answer isn't always the most obvious one. I'm interested in how that same kind of thinking applies to business, technology, and the decisions people make.

Outside the classroom, I've worked in sports operations, media production, and student leadership. As Equipment Manager and Field Coordinator with Allen Sports Association, I helped coordinate equipment and field operations supporting more than 500 youth athletes, working with coaches, staff, and volunteers to keep things running smoothly. Managing equipment valued at over $20,000 and coordinating field setups taught me to plan ahead, manage details, and solve practical problems when several things need attention at once.

Through Youth and Government, including serving as president and previously as a communications officer, I've worked on growing participation, coordinating a team, and helping turn shared goals into concrete results. I helped contribute to a program that grew from around 30 members to more than 100, with teams earning district and state recognition.

My experience in podcast production gave me a different perspective on planning and communication. Working across episode planning, guest coordination, production, and promotion showed me how different pieces of a project fit together. Through DECA financial analysis and case competitions, I've also practiced evaluating information, building recommendations, and explaining the reasoning behind a decision.

At UT Dallas, I'm building on these experiences through Business Analytics and Artificial Intelligence. I'm especially interested in business strategy, data-driven decisions, emerging technology, and how analytical thinking can help turn complicated ideas into something useful in the real world.
